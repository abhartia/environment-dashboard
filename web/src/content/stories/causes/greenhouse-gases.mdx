of Oceanography. NOAA has measured there since May 1974.<Cite id="scripps-co2" /><Cite id="noaa-gml-trends" />

## Two thousand years of air in ice

Snow falling on Antarctica traps small bubbles of air as it turns to ice. Ice cores drilled at Law Dome hold air from
the last two thousand years, and today's level is higher than any year in that record.<Cite id="law-dome-2k" />
